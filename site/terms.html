What NurseFlow is

NurseFlow is a planning aid that helps a charge nurse set up a floor, add nurses and patients, and plan shift assignments. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor or make clinical decisions.

Assignments are suggestions

The assignments NurseFlow produces are suggestions. The charge nurse’s own judgment decides who cares for which patients. Do not rely on NurseFlow to decide patient care.

Dummy data only

Use NurseFlow with made-up information only. Do not enter real patient information, such as real patient initials, ages, diagnoses or room details, and do not put patient details in messages. Use first names only for nurses.

Provided as-is during testing

NurseFlow is a test version, provided as-is. It may contain mistakes, change, lose data or stop working at any time.

Your account

You can delete your account in the app. See the Privacy Policy for how your information is handled.
