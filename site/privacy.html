NurseFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: EFFECTIVE DATE

Dummy data only. NurseFlow is a demo. Do not enter real patient information.

About this app

NurseFlow is a test version of an app that helps charge nurses plan shift assignments. It is shared with invited testers through Apple’s TestFlight. It is meant to be used with made-up data only. Real patient information is not allowed, and the app is not meant for use with real patients.

What we collect and why

  • Account email. To create your account and sign you in. Email confirmation is turned off in this demo, so an email address on an account may not have been verified as belonging to the person who signed up.
  • Password. To sign you in. It is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and is not stored in readable form.
  • Display name. To show who you are in the app.
  • Floor and shift setup. Floor names, rooms, beds, doctor sides and shift details you enter, so the app can plan assignments.
  • Patient fields. Patient initials, age, sex, diagnosis and bed acuity, if you enter them. These are used to plan assignments. Enter made-up values only.
  • Staff names. The names, license types, experience levels and maximum patient loads of nurses a charge nurse adds. Use first names only.
  • Requests and messages. Issue and swap requests, and the messages in them. Do not include patient details.

We collect this only to run the app. We do not sell it or use it for anything else.

No advertising, tracking or analytics

NurseFlow has no advertising, no tracking, and no outside analytics services. Apple’s TestFlight may give the developer crash reports and tester feedback. That is handled by Apple under its own policies.

This version does not send push notifications, so it does not collect a device notification token.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and Google Cloud (the app’s server), on servers in the United States.

Who can see what

  • You can see the data you create.
  • A charge nurse can see the floors, shifts, nurse names, patient fields, assignments, requests and messages for the shifts they run.
  • A nurse who joins a shift with a code from a charge nurse can see the floor name, their own assignment (their assigned rooms and beds, including the patient fields on those beds) and the requests and messages they take part in.
  • The developer can see everything stored, through the database and server dashboards, and uses that access to run the demo, fix problems and answer requests from you.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except the service providers that store it, named above.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data until your account is deleted, including floors and shifts that have ended. Ending a shift does not delete it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. This removes the data you own, including your messages. A charge nurse’s floor templates and shifts are removed too, including for nurses on those shifts.

If you cannot sign in, email pratham.sitoula03@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for the account to be deleted. There is no password reset in this demo, so the same address can be used to ask for help getting back in.

Seeing or correcting your data

To ask what we hold about you, or to have something corrected, email pratham.sitoula03@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
